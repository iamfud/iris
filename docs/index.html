Dynamic Stream Deck for your PC

Turn your PC into a command centre.

Iris is a dynamic, Stream Deck–style utility that puts your whole setup under one roof — controlling and automating lighting, running a screen-capture toolbar with annotations and OCR, mixing media and per-app volumes, tracking PC statistics, and driving custom button boxes in games and simulators. Add a conditional macro system with hotkey injection, a vision system with OCR, and a library browser for every capture and note you take. Fully extensible with plugins — including Home Assistant.

Features

Everything under one roof

Iris is a dynamic Stream Deck–style utility. Assign anything to a button — control lighting, capture & annotate your screen, mix audio, run shots, and react to your PC, your games, and your smart home.

🎛️

Control & Automate Lighting

Drive RGB and smart-home lighting from buttons, OpenRGB presets, and reactive automations (e.g. lighting turns red when you enter combat).

📸

Capture Toolbar + OCR

A transient screen-capture toolbar — fullscreen or zone screenshots, pixel annotations, and automatic OCR text extraction to your clipboard, all filed in the library.

🎨

Color Picker & Notepad

A system-wide color picker (with pixel grab and clipboard HEX) and a built-in notepad with quick notes — everything collected in one integrated library browser.

🔊

Media Mixing & App Volume

Full media transport controls plus per-application volume mixing — adjust the currently active app's volume and mute from a button or slider.

📊

Live PC Statistics

CPU/GPU temperature, usage, and FPS. Stream to the optional MAX7219 matrix display, the floating overlay, or your phone panel.

🎮

Game / App Button Boxes

Display custom button boxes inside simulators, games, or apps — per-game actions and telemetry via integrations like Elite Dangerous.

👁️

Vision System + OCR

Screen-analysis sensors watch a region and fire events on colour conditions — plus full OCR for extracting text from anything on screen.

⚡

Conditional Macros & Hotkeys

A macro system with hotkey injection and reactive events. Trigger actions when a condition is true — e.g. change lighting when combat starts.

🏠

Home Assistant & Plugins

Control lights, scenes, and switches; extend with addons and plugins including Home Assistant, OpenRGB, Elite Dangerous, and PC stats.

Hardware

What you'll need

Iris runs on an ESP8266/ESP32 plus a MAX7219 LED matrix, connected over USB serial.

📟

Display

MAX7219 LED matrix, FC16_HW type — 4 daisy-chained 8×8 modules.

🔌

Microcontroller

ESP8266 (Wemos D1 Mini) or ESP32, flashed with the bundled firmware.

🖥️

PC

Windows 10/11 with Python 3.10+ running the Iris desktop app.

Components

An integrated command system

Iris is a full utility stack — desktop app, capture & library tools, macro engine, hardware display, and a control panel.

🗔

Desktop App

Python app with a system tray, floating overlay, and all the data plumbing.

📱

Phone Panel / PWA

A web panel (installable as a PWA) for pairing and controlling Iris from your phone.

🤖

Android App

A native Android companion in android/ for companion-device use.

📚

Library Browser

One integrated browser for every screenshot, OCR text, and note — grouped per app, searchable from the panel.

🧩

Plugin System

Extensible plugins for Elite Dangerous, OpenRGB, Home Assistant, PC stats, lighting, and vision sensors.

⚡

Macro & Automation Engine

Conditional macros, hotkey injection, and reactive events that fire actions (lighting, hotkeys, notifications) based on live state.

Pair your device

This panel only allows access from devices paired by scanning its QR code. There is no password.

1Open the Iris panel on your PC and go to Settings → Network.
2Scan the QR code at the top right with your phone camera.
3Your phone is added to the paired devices list and opens the panel automatically.

Pairing only works on your local network. A device stays paired until you revoke it from the PC.
